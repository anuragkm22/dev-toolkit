What this site does

All tools on Dev Toolkit run entirely client-side, in your browser. Anything you type or paste into a tool (such as JSON or YAML content) is processed locally and is never transmitted to a server, stored in a database, or logged by us. There is no backend.

Shareable links

Some tools let you generate a shareable link. The data for that link is encoded directly into the URL itself (after the # symbol), not sent to or stored on a server. Anyone with the link can view the encoded content — treat share links the same way you'd treat any other file you're sharing.

Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics (GA4) to understand overall traffic — which pages get visited and roughly how many people use each tool. Google Analytics may set cookies and collect information such as your approximate location (city/region level, derived from IP address), device type, browser, and referring page. This is separate from the tools themselves: analytics tracks that you visited a page, not what you typed or pasted into any tool, which never leaves your browser.

You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking using a browser extension such as the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on, or by using your browser's tracking-protection or ad-blocking features.

Advertising

This site may display advertising (e.g. Google AdSense) in the future to support hosting costs. Advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to serve relevant ads. This section will be updated with specifics once that integration is added.
